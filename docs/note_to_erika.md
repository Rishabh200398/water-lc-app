For my AI final project I want to build something we'd actually use: an app that takes the busywork out of running Water LC. I'd love your take before I start building this weekend.

**The idea in one line:** an assistant that runs our year with us (budget, approvals, memory and the newsletter), not just one event at a time.

**What it would do**

1. **Plan an event from one starting point.** We type a theme and a possible speaker. It gives back a logistics checklist (room, AV, timeline, speaker communication) and drafts the speaker invitation.
2. **Build the budget for us.** It works out the speaker's travel, hotel and honorarium, plus lunch from our actual vendor sheet, based on expected RSVPs and dietary needs. It shows how much of the year's budget is left before and after. Spending limits are hard rules in the app, so the AI can't talk its way past them.
3. **Prepare one clean approval request for Jim.** Instead of back-and-forth emails, Jim gets a single itemized summary to approve or reject. Nothing gets ordered or sent until he says yes, and every decision is logged.
4. **Remember everything.** Every event records its speaker, vendor, cost per person and turnout. Next time we plan, it can tell us things like "last lunch from this vendor was $14 a head for 35 people."
5. **Write the newsletter from what already happened.** The yearly newsletter (and a year-end summary for Jim) is drafted from the events, speakers and attendance the app already has, so we're not rebuilding the year from memory.
6. **Help with marketing, if time allows.** Poster text, the announcement email and social posts, drafted from the same event details.

**Why it's useful for us**

- **Less time per event.** No more digging through five spreadsheets in Drive or rewriting the same emails.
- **No budget surprises.** We always know where we stand, and Jim sees exactly what he's approving.
- **Easier for Jim.** One clear request per event instead of scattered threads.
- **A real handoff.** When we graduate, the next co-leads inherit the history: speakers, vendors, costs and what worked. They won't start from zero.
- **Bigger than Water LC.** Every student group and learning community at Yale has the same problem, so this could help others too.

- A quick look at whether these are the right pain points, and what I'm missing.
- Help exporting our budget and vendor spreadsheets (I can swap in fake numbers).
- After I demo it, a short quote on whether it would save us time. It would help a lot with my grade!
