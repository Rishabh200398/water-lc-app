Connecticut towns expand PFAS testing of private wells

HARTFORD - Several Connecticut towns announced this month that they will offer free PFAS testing for private wells, after state data showed elevated levels near former industrial sites. Officials said results will be shared with homeowners within six weeks and that filters will be subsidized for affected households.
