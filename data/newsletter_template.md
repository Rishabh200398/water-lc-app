# Water LC monthly newsletter: template

Taken from the September and October 2026 issues (sent by email from the Yale Connect portal).
The app fills in each section as ready-to-paste text. A co-lead copies it into the portal.

## Style
- Warm, upbeat, student voice, with light water puns ("going swimmingly").
- Signed "Erika and Rishabh, Water LC co-leads". A personal note can be signed "--Rishabh".
- Each item is a short bold title line plus 2–5 sentences. Links appear as "Register here", "Read the full article here", "Apply here".
- Don't publish students' emails or personal details unless they agreed to it.

## Sections (in order)

1. **Greeting**: "Hello Water Learning Community!" plus 2–4 sentences on the season and what's in this issue.
   (The first issue of the year also introduces the co-leads and the advisor, Prof. Jim Saiers.)
2. **Event Recaps**: one paragraph per past event: what happened, who hosted or partnered, a thank-you to attendees and speakers. Mention co-hosts (e.g. "Fresh & Salty SIG x Connecticut Culture Club").
3. **Upcoming Events**: for each event: title, date (and time and room if known), "Hosted by: X x Water LC", a 2–3 sentence description, whether lunch is provided, and a "Register here" link.
4. **Water in the News**: subsections **Global**, **National**, and **Local (Connecticut)** when available. Each is one story summarized in 3–5 sentences with a link to the article.
5. **Spotlight**: 1–2 positive stories (innovation, restoration, stewardship pledges). Good news only.
6. **Helpful Links**: mostly the same every month.
   - Newsletters and Podcasts: USGS WaterMarks Newsletter; Aquafornia Newsletter; (Don't) Waste Water Podcast
   - Organizations: American Water Works Association (AWWA); CUAHSI
7. **Upcoming Conferences**: "Name - dates (City, ST)" plus a 2–4 sentence description. Drop conferences whose dates have passed.
8. **Job & Internship Postings**: opens with "Be sure to keep an eye out for new job listings on Josh's Water Jobs!" Each posting is one line: "**Title**: Organization (City, ST). Apply here by <date>." or "Rolling applications - more information here." Sorted by deadline, rolling applications last.
