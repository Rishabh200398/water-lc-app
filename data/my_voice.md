# How Rishabh and Erika write emails (style guide for drafts)

- Warm, direct and short. 120-180 words for a first email.
- Open with who we are in one line: "We're Rishabh and Erika, co-leads of the Water Learning Community at the Yale School of the Environment."
- Say why *this* person: one specific line about their work or career path.
- The ask is concrete: a short call (15-20 minutes) to talk about a Lunch and Learn / talk, and 2-3 possible weeks or a request for their availability. Virtual is welcome; in person is welcome too.
- Plain language, no jargon, no flattery overload. One exclamation mark at most.
- Mention students' interest briefly (for example "water quality was the top request from our members this fall").
- Close: "Thanks so much, and we hope to connect soon!" then "Rishabh and Erika" / "Water LC co-leads, Yale School of the Environment".
- Never promise money, a specific honorarium, a room or a date that isn't confirmed.
